# Sparkle & Shine Home Services Knowledge Base

## 1. About Sparkle & Shine
Welcome to Sparkle & Shine Home Services! We are a family-owned business dedicated to making your home a cleaner, brighter, and happier place to live. Founded in 2010, we started as a small neighborhood cleaning crew and have since grown to offer a full suite of home maintenance services. Our goal is to give you back your free time by handling the chores you don't want to do. All our team members are fully background-checked, insured, and trained to treat your home with the utmost respect. We pride ourselves on our punctuality, friendly smiles, and our signature "white glove" attention to detail.

## 2. Standard Home Cleaning Services
Our Standard Cleaning service is designed for regular upkeep to keep your home looking fresh. This service includes dusting all accessible surfaces, wiping down kitchen counters and appliances (exterior only), cleaning and sanitizing bathrooms (including toilets, sinks, and tubs), vacuuming all carpets and rugs, and mopping all hard floors. We also empty all trash cans and make the beds if fresh linens are left out. We offer this service on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule. Please note that standard cleaning does not include moving heavy furniture or scrubbing inside ovens or refrigerators.

## 3. Deep Cleaning Services
Sometimes your home needs a little extra love, which is where our Deep Cleaning service comes in. We recommend this for first-time customers or as an annual spring-cleaning event. In addition to everything in our Standard Cleaning, the Deep Cleaning includes hand-washing baseboards, wiping down ceiling fans and light fixtures, scrubbing grout lines in bathrooms and kitchens, cleaning the interior of the microwave, and dusting inside window sills and blinds. This process takes significantly longer than a standard clean and requires a dedicated team of at least two professionals.

## 4. Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning
Moving is stressful enough without worrying about cleaning! Our Move-In/Move-Out service is specially designed for empty homes. We clean the home from top to bottom so it's completely ready for the next residents. This package includes everything in the Deep Cleaning service, plus wiping down the inside of all empty cabinets and drawers, cleaning the interior of the refrigerator and oven, and removing cobwebs from high corners. We guarantee that your landlord or the new buyers will be thrilled with the cleanliness of the space.

## 5. Lawn Care and Landscaping Services
Beyond the inside of your home, we also take care of the outside! Our basic Lawn Care package includes weekly lawn mowing, edge trimming along driveways and walkways, and blowing grass clippings off paved surfaces. For seasonal needs, we offer Fall Leaf Cleanup (raking, bagging, and removal of dead leaves) and Spring Prep (mulching, weed pulling, and planting new seasonal flowers). We also provide light tree and shrub trimming to keep your garden looking neat. We do not handle large tree removals or heavy hardscaping (like building patios or retaining walls).

## 6. Basic Handyman and Maintenance Services
Got a leaky faucet or a squeaky door? Our Handyman team is here to help with minor household repairs. We can patch drywall holes, paint single rooms, hang pictures or TVs on the wall, assemble flat-pack furniture (like IKEA), replace light fixtures, and fix running toilets. For safety and licensing reasons, our handymen do not perform major plumbing overhauls, full electrical rewiring, or structural roof repairs. If a job is beyond our scope, we are happy to recommend a licensed specialist from our trusted network.

## 7. Pricing and Payment
We believe in simple and transparent pricing. Cleaning services are typically billed at a flat rate based on the square footage of your home and the number of bedrooms and bathrooms. For example, a standard clean for a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home usually starts at $150. Handyman and landscaping services are billed at an hourly rate of $75 per hour, plus the cost of any materials needed for the job. We require a credit card on file to book an appointment. Your card will only be charged after the service is completed. We also accept cash and personal checks upon arrival.

## 8. Scheduling, Cancellations, and Rescheduling
Life happens, and we understand that schedules change! We ask that you provide at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. If you cancel with less than 48 hours' notice, a $50 late-cancellation fee will be charged to your card on file. If our team arrives at your home and cannot get inside (e.g., you forgot to leave the key or the door code changed), a lockout fee of $75 will apply. You can easily manage your schedule, skip a week, or request a different time through our online customer portal.

## 9. Pet Policy
We love pets! You do not need to lock your pets away while we work, provided they are friendly and comfortable around strangers and vacuums. However, for the safety of our staff, if your dog is aggressive or highly anxious, we kindly ask that they be crated or kept in a separate room during the service. Please note that our teams are not equipped to clean up pet waste (feces, urine, or vomit) or empty litter boxes due to health and safety regulations.

## 10. Satisfaction Guarantee and Damage Policy
Your satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a 100% Happiness Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with our work, please call us within 24 hours of the service, and we will send a team back to re-clean the missed areas completely free of charge. While our teams are incredibly careful, accidents occasionally happen. If a team member accidentally breaks an item in your home, we are fully bonded and insured. Please notify us immediately, and we will arrange for the item to be repaired or replaced up to a value of $2,000.

## 11. Eco-Friendly and Green Cleaning Options
We care about the environment and the health of your family. Upon request, we offer a "Green Clean" package. Instead of traditional harsh chemicals, our team will use 100% eco-friendly, non-toxic, and biodegradable cleaning products. These products are completely safe for children, pets, and allergy sufferers. There is a small $15 surcharge for the Green Clean package due to the higher cost of these specialized materials. Just let us know when booking if you prefer the green option!
